[ durable log ]

Every fleet message is also written to disk

fleet/# is a durable topic. Its messages go to an append-only, CRC-checked, segmented log. A writer thread appends everything that's waiting, then pays for one fsync per batch (group commit).

Why batch: on an SSD one real fsync costs ~3.4 ms, so syncing every record caps the log at ~290 msg/s. Group commit of 1024 reaches ~258k msg/s.

[ backpressure ]

One slow subscriber can't slow anyone else down.

This starts a subscriber that never reads, then floods its topic. Its kernel buffers fill first, then its 256-frame queue in the broker. After that the broker drops messages for that subscriber only, and after 500 drops in a row it disconnects it. Watch the fleet and the chart above: they keep flowing the whole time.

Runs for a few seconds. One demo at a time across all visitors.
